MANANG · 3,500 MANNAPURNA REGION · 2019
Why Yachu

Out there, you feel the weight of everything you carry.

That's me in Manang in 2019, above 3,500 m. On that ride, like on most of my trips, my gear weighed more than it needed to, and my back felt every gram.

While many leave to chase a dream abroad, I stayed to chase the Nepali dream.

In 2023 I started Yachu to make outdoor gear that's light, simple and made well.

Yachu (याचु) is my grandmother's name. In Newari, it means clear and pure.

Our mission: help people get out there more, slow down, worry less and leave no trace.

Our vision: make quality outdoor gear in Nepal, earn people's trust, and take it to the world.

We're just getting started, and we're grateful for supporters all over the world. Still learning, still building, one piece at a time.

Bishal Shrestha Founder, Yachu My notes: bishal.sh

How to hang your hammock in 60 seconds

The Airlite Hammock Set goes up without a single knot. Here's how to do it in about a minute.

What you need

Everything comes in the pouch: the hammock, two tree straps and two carabiners. All you have to find is two strong supports or trees.

Three steps

  1. 01Wrap the straps. One around each strong support or tree.
  2. 02Clip in. The carabiners come attached to the hammock. Clip each one to a strap.
  3. 03Adjust and lie back. Pull the whoopie slings to set a 30° sag. Lie slightly diagonal to sleep flat.

Before you get in

Hang it at a height that's safe if you fall, high enough that it won't touch the ground when you lie in it. Max load is 160 kg. Not for swinging or jumping.
